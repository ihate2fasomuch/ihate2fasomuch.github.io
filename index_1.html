How to play

Open any section. Hover any card or unit for its details.
1The goal

Push the front (the bar at the top) all the way to the enemy's end. You move it by winning rounds.

The twist: you never choose your own cards. Every card you fight with was stocked by your opponent — and every card they fight with was stocked by you.

2A round, step by step
  1. Supply. You see a shelf of 25 of the enemy's cards. Put exactly 8 into each of two packs (each needs at least one unit). Prices are fixed by the round: 6, 9, 12… You also choose one permanent upgrade to list for them, and set its price.
  2. Market. You buy one of their two packs; the other is thrown away. Spend leftover meta on a tonic, sticker crates, your workshop, or the upgrade they listed for you.
  3. Combat. Five turns. Units and tricks cost sun (Grove) or spore (Blight), which refills every turn and does not carry over.
  4. Round end. Whoever holds more main lanes moves the front one step — two if they win by 3 or more.
3Stocking packs — building a trap

You can't hold good cards back: both packs must be full. If they refuse both packs they keep their meta, but Rubble goes into their own deck. So the real lever is selling cards that are strong alone and useless together:

Strand a payoff

Auras and scalers (Fungal Chorus, Bristlecap…) have 0 attack. Sold without their tribe, they do nothing.

Clog the curve

Resource doesn't stack. A pack of 5-drops can't all be cast.

Mistime a window

Turn-1-only and turn-4+ cards are bricks in the wrong deck.

Split a pair

Cross-tribe cards want a partner tribe. Sell one half.

They are doing the same to you. Each pack shows its tricks, average cost and dead weight for free; appraising (2 meta) adds a rough synergy read and the tribe it leans toward.

4Combat turns
  1. The first mover commits units without seeing the other side. Normally that is Blight.
  2. The second mover answers with units and tricks, seeing everything.
  3. The first mover casts tricks last. Committing blind is the cost; the last word is the reward.

If the front is pushed deep into one side's home, that side takes the first-mover role.

The exchange: in each lane, all your units' attack adds up and hits their front unit; overkill carries to the next one. Both sides hit at once. So a tough unit in front shields everything behind it.

Once per turn you may scrap a card from your hand for 2 resource.

5Capturing lanes

Each lane has a capture bar. If only your units are left standing in a lane, each one pushes the bar by attack × health per turn (a 2/2 pushes 4%, a 6/6 pushes 36%). Lanes with units from both sides don't move.

20% — held

Counts toward winning the round and pays resource.

60% — captured

Your strongest unit there stays as a garrison next round. A captured flank also rakes the main lane beside it and speeds your capture there.

Flanks

Holding a flank sector at round end pays +3 meta and breaks ties on main lanes. Flanks move 1.6× faster and get random terrain. On the wide map, hold 2 of a sector's 3.

Backs to the wall

Units fight +1 in a lane the enemy holds, +2 once it is captured.

6Your deck between rounds

Draw pile

You draw through a shuffled pile; cards only come back once you've drawn everything.

Cutting

3 meta, +3 for each extra cut that round, and every real card you cut makes future cuts +1. Rubble can always be cut.

Tonics

Three offered, take one per round. Changes that card in your deck forever — every copy. Each one you buy makes the next 2 dearer.

Grove cross-breeds

Two Thornlings → a Bramblejack; two Bramblejacks → a Thornwarden. These Hybrids drop a tier when damaged instead of dying.

Blight experiments

Two cards of one tribe + 4 meta → a random Abomination. 2 meta to reroll.

Graft

Three cards of one tribe + 5 meta → one big body that keeps the tribe.

7Sticker book

A crate costs 5 meta and gives three pieces from 15 sets.

Perk sets

Every piece is a level that works straight away — more meta each round, cheaper appraisals, fewer refusal Rubble, and so on.

Companion sets

One piece short of complete, a rookie companion (−1/−1) joins; complete, it's full strength. Deploy one free per turn, each once per round.

8What carries over

Units clear between rounds. Three things carry:

Veterancy ★

A card still standing at round end PERMANENTLY gets +1/+1 — up to +3 for cheap cards, +2 for 3–4 costs, +1 for big and workshop cards.

Garrison

Your strongest unit in each captured lane starts next round there.

Reserve

Up to three unplayed cards stay in your hand.

PERMANENTLY always means the card in your deck (veterancy, tonics, Consume, scaling cards). Every other buff lasts until the round ends.
